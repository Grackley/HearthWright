# Changelog

## 1.2.1 — Correct PNG map scale

- Corrected full-world Image Only PNG calibration to a 24,576-meter span: 3 meters per pixel for an 8192 × 8192 image.
- Applied the corrected calibration automatically to new and saved plans, including older map metadata, without changing piece or annotation geometry.
- Documented the distinction between full-world PNG images and the separate All Data export.

## 1.2.0 — First community release

- Plan with 397 build pieces from Valheim 1.0.7, organized into 14 catalog sections.
- Browse Timberwood, Scalewood walls, Ice, drawbridges, 67° roofs and supports, furniture, and crafting pieces.
- Place pieces individually, in aligned lines, or across rectangular areas with snapping and free placement.
- Add and reorder building levels, with visibility controls and undo/redo.
- Move and rotate selections together and inspect their combined material requirements.
- View comfort, spawn-blocking, and crafting-station range overlays.
- Import world PNGs, mark cultivated ground, and add text or drawings.
- Save editable `.hearthwright` projects and export PNG images.
- Learn the controls through a centered, replayable walkthrough.

The public source history begins with this release.
